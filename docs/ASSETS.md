## Before publishing

For every **⚠️** row, record one of the following here:

* *"Drawn by <name>, released under <license>"*;
* *"Generated with <tool>; that tool's terms allow redistribution and commercial use"* (check
  them, they differ); or
* *"Licensed from <source>, license allows redistribution in open-source apps"* with a link.

If you can't confirm a file, replace it before the repository goes public. A missing image
never crashes the app: course icons fall back to SF Symbols, and the other decorations
simply don't appear.

Never add downloaded course PDFs, textbook pages or other third-party material to the repository.
